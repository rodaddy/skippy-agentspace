# Generate Explanation Artifact

**Purpose:** Assemble interview answers into a clean, portfolio-ready explanation artifact.

**When to Use:**
- After completing the four-phase interview loop
- When user has existing notes they want formatted as an artifact
- When user says "generate artifact", "format as artifact"

---

## Assembly Rules

### Voice & Tone
- First person, user's voice -- not the interviewer's
- Practitioner explaining to a sharp peer
- Dense with specifics, honest about limitations
- Clear about reasoning behind every choice
- NO self-congratulation, NO marketing, NO filler

### Accuracy
- Use ONLY information the user actually provided
- Do not invent details or add technical specifics they didn't mention
- If understanding was shallow in places, the artifact reflects that
- A real artifact with visible gaps > polished fiction

### Length
- Each section: 2-5 sentences of dense, specific, honest writing
- Total: fits on roughly one page

---

## Artifact Format

```markdown
## Explanation Artifact: [Project Name]

**What is this**
[2-5 sentences. What it literally does, what problem it addresses, who has that problem.
First person. Specific. No marketing.]

**Why this approach**
[2-5 sentences. Alternatives considered, tradeoffs evaluated, what was deliberately excluded.
Where AI contributed vs. where you made judgment calls. What you chose NOT to build and why.]

**What would break**
[2-5 sentences. Fragile points, dependencies, assumptions, edge cases.
Honest gaps in understanding. Written with the specificity of someone who has
actually thought about failure modes.]

**What I learned**
[2-5 sentences. Concrete discoveries, mistakes, corrections.
What you'd do differently. Not platitudes -- real cognitive change.]
```

---

## Post-Assembly

1. Present the artifact to the user
2. Ask: "Read through this. Does it accurately capture your understanding? Is there anything you want to adjust, sharpen, or be more honest about?"
3. Make any requested adjustments
4. Deliver final version
5. Note: "This artifact is ready to attach to your project -- portfolio, README, TalentBoard, wherever your work lives. The proof of understanding travels with the work itself."
6. Offer next steps: "Want me to generate resume bullets from this? Update your comprehension ledger?"
